Kotoba Miner v2.2 — Read this first

Update your existing Opera extension:
1. Back up your existing Kotoba folder.
2. Unzip this archive. Open its single Kotoba-Miner-v2.2 folder.
3. Copy all files *inside* it into your existing loaded extension folder (replace old files).
4. Open opera://extensions and click Reload on Kotoba.
5. Refresh your video tabs.
6. On YouTube or Netflix, click the Opera toolbar Kotoba extension icon and Enable the site. On other video websites, approve the site-specific permission when asked.

New video sites start DISABLED. The floating 語 button is visible only on sites you enable. Existing explicit approvals are preserved.
